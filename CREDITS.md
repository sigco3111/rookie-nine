## Motion data
Batting swing and pitching motion are derived from CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database, subject 124 (http://mocap.cs.cmu.edu). The database states: "free for all uses"; may be included in commercial products, but the data may not be resold directly, even in converted form. Converted joint-position clips are in src/mocap/ (converter: tools/cmu2json.py). Data is provided as-is.
